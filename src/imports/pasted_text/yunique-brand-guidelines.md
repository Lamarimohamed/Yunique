6. HERO / INTRO EXPERIENCE
==================================================

THIS IS ONE OF THE MOST IMPORTANT PARTS.

Create a cinematic opening experience.

When the website first loads:

The screen starts almost completely black.

A subtle fingerprint pattern appears in the darkness.

The fingerprint should NOT simply fade in.

Animate the fingerprint as if it is being DRAWN / SCANNED / REVEALED.

Animation sequence:

1. Black screen.

2. Tiny white fingerprint lines begin appearing.

3. Lines progressively reveal themselves.

4. The fingerprint expands very subtly.

5. Some fingerprint lines move with a slight fluid distortion.

6. The YUNIQUE logo emerges from the fingerprint.

7. The fingerprint briefly becomes the dominant visual.

8. The fingerprint contracts / shifts toward the background.

9. Main homepage hero appears.

The animation should feel:

TECHNICAL
HUMAN
PREMIUM
MYSTERIOUS
CINEMATIC

Do NOT make it feel like a loading spinner.

Allow reduced-motion accessibility.
